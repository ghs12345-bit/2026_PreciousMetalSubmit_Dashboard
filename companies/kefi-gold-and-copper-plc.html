1Overview
  • KEFI Gold and Copper plc is an AIM-listed gold and copper development company focused on the Arabian-Nubian Shield, with its most advanced asset the Tulu Kapi gold project in western Ethiopia (construction started) and a growth portfolio in Saudi Arabia via a 13%-owned JV, Gold & Minerals Co. (GMCO).
  • Tulu Kapi hosts a Probable Ore Reserve of 1.05 Moz Au and Mineral Resources of 1.72 Moz Au at 2.65 g/t, underpinned by a 2017 DFS Update supporting a 1.9–2.1 Mtpa CIL plant.
  • In Saudi Arabia, GMCO's Jibal Qutman (0.90 Moz Au MRE, Feasibility Started) and the Hawiah VMS deposit (38.2 Mt Cu-Au-Zn-Ag) represent a longer-term, larger gold-equivalent pipeline.
Strategic positioning
  • Tulu Kapi financing fully assembled (>US$400m: US$240m secured debt + US$60m contractor + US$100m+ equity); debt drawdown targeted Q4 2026.
  • KEFI intends to keep Tulu Kapi as the execution focus while the GMCO pipeline self-funds Saudi growth via joint venturing.
