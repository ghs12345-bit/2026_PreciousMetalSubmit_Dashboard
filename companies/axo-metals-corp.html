1Overview
  • Axo Metals Corp. (renamed from Axo Copper Corp. effective 2026-05-01, ticker unchanged) is a Halifax-headquartered explorer-developer with two Mexican assets and a 30 June fiscal year-end.
  • The company IPO'd on the TSX-V in June 2025 as a single-asset copper explorer, then transformed itself in January 2026 by acquiring the past-producing San Antonio gold project in Sonora from Osisko Development.
  • The acquisition was funded and de-risked in quick succession: a bought deal that escalated from C$25M to C$35M to a final C$40.25M closed 2026-02-19, leaving C$36.6M of cash at 2026-03-31.
  • San Antonio is now the value driver — a brownfield restart with a 2021-built 4,000 tpd heap-leach/CIC plant, an approved MIA, and a PEA in progress. La Huerta is the exploration call option.
  • Strategy is explicitly staged: use existing infrastructure to return San Antonio to gold production at low capex, while drilling to grow both the gold resource and the La Huerta copper discovery.
How the two assets differ
  • San Antonio: 43 concessions over 11,338 ha, five deposits, ~85,000 m of historical drilling across 584 holes, a NI 43-101 resource, and a permitted plant — but a 2022-vintage estimate and no economic study.
  • La Huerta: ~11,331 ha optioned under the La Gallina Rights Option Agreement (1,000,000 shares issued annually), high-grade copper dykes over ~5 km of strike, and explicitly no mineral resource — Item 14 of the January 2025 technical report reads 'not applicable'.
  • Both sit in established Mexican mining states (Sonora, Jalisco) with the board deliberately including two Mexican nationals, one of whom is Director General of CAMIMEX, the Mexican Mining Chamber.
What the market is actually pricing
  • At C$183.8M market cap the equity is valued on ~1.12 Moz of Au resource plus the La Huerta discovery, with no study-level economics available.
  • The June 2026 deck computes enterprise value as market cap less cash (C$147.5M), which excludes the C$74.3M stream liability and C$19.3M ARO. Adjusted for those, the economic EV is materially higher.
  • The PEA is therefore the single most important disclosure event: it will convert the story from asset-quality assertion into modelled cash flow, and will reset the resource to a current gold price.

  • La Huerta geology: bornite and chalcopyrite in intrusive-related dykes cutting andesite, traced ~5 km at surface, with alteration halos up to 30 m wide; drilling to date is shallow and concentrated within 1 km of the Las Marias historic workings.

5Company Profile
  • Halifax, Nova Scotia head office; TSX-V listed under AXO; 30 June fiscal year-end; renamed from Axo Copper Corp. to Axo Metals Corp. effective 2026-05-01 following an 87% pivot in asset mix.

  • Consideration for San Antonio was share-heavy and milestone-deferred rather than cash: 15,305,536 shares to Osisko Development (~9.99% of basic shares at the time) plus 70% of any Mexican VAT refunds and US$2M each on feasibility study and first gold pour.

  • Both Osisko entities took 12-month lock-ups, gave 24-month voting support, and hold anti-dilution rights on the company's first US$10M financing.

Axo Metals Secures Major Environmental Permit Approval; Clears Path For Production At San Antonio
