1Overview
  • Luca Mining Corp. (TSX-V: LUCA; OTCQX: LUCMF; Frankfurt: Z68) is a Mexican precious- and base-metals producer operating two mines: Campo Morado (polymetallic Zn-Pb-Cu-Au-Ag) in Guerrero and Tahuehueto (Au-Ag) in Durango.
  • The Company produces gold, silver, zinc, copper and lead, providing diversified metal revenue and operating cash flow.
  • Strategy focuses on optimising current operations while advancing near-mine exploration and resource growth across its Sierra Madre land package.
Production profile
  • Campo Morado operates at ~2,300 tpd and is among Mexico's largest zinc producers.
  • Tahuehueto achieved commercial production in March 2025, averaging ~1,115 tpd milled in Q2 2026 (above its 1,000 tpd installed nameplate).
2Core Assets
  • Campo Morado (Guerrero): polymetallic VMS. M&I 16,627,000 t @ 8.68% ZnEq (Zn 4.01%, Pb 0.93%, Cu 0.80%, Au 1.70 g/t, Ag 123 g/t); Inferred 998,000 t @ 7.27% ZnEq; cut-off 5.5% ZnEq.
  • Tahuehueto (Durango): Au-Ag epithermal. Proven+Probable reserves 3.77 Mt (294 koz Au, 5.77 Moz Ag); M&I resource 6.26 Mt (425 koz Au, 9.45 Moz Ag); Inferred 0.92 Mt (30 koz Au). PFS dated Apr 27, 2022.
  • Exploration assets El Realito and Jocuixtita (Sinaloa) extend the Mexican land package; Tahuehueto hosts ~11 km of prospective vein strike versus ~4.5 km in the current resource model.
