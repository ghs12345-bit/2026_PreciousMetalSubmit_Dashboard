Key risks
  • No mineral resource of any category.   • Both major partner arrangements have unwound.   • Assay throughput is the binding constraint on news flow. As at 9 July 2026 only 4 of 27 completed holes had full assays back, with the company publicly citing 'extreme volumes' and worsening delays at sample-prep and assay laboratories - a 40,000m program can outrun the ability to report it.
  • Single-jurisdiction, single-asset concentration in Kazakhstan, plus governance overlap: the CEO and CFO both hold the same offices at Silver Bull Resources (from which Arras was reorganised in 2021) and the Chairman also chairs Silver Bull - attention and related-party dynamics warrant monitoring. Reported grades are core lengths with true widths unknown, and CuEq/AuEq figures use company price and recovery assumptions that were retroactively restated in August 2025.

1Overview
  • Arras Minerals Corp. (TSX-V: ARK, OTCQB: ARRKF) is a Vancouver-headquartered copper-gold exploration company whose entire operating footprint is in northeastern Kazakhstan. It was incorporated on 5 February 2021 under the BC Business Corporations Act as part of an asset-purchase reorganisation of Silver Bull Resources, and on 3 February 2022 acquired 100% of Ekidos Minerals LLP, the Kazakh entity through which all field operations are conducted.
  • The company holds more than 3,300 km2 of exploration licences roughly 70 km southwest of Pavlodar and about 300 km northeast of Astana, inside the Central Asian Orogenic Belt - the same accretionary orogen that hosts Muruntau in Uzbekistan and Oyu Tolgoi in Mongolia. Note that the company's own website quotes 'more than 3,000' on one page and 'more than 3,300' on another; the corporate deck uses 3,300 km2.
  • The flagship is the Elemes copper-gold porphyry project - two exploration licences covering approximately 530 km2 (the 9 July 2026 release specifies 531 km2), located 13-20 km southwest of Ekibastuz (population ~125,000) and about 20 km from Arras's operational base.
  • Within under 18 months of first drilling, three separate porphyry centres have been identified along the ~10 km Berezski Trend: Berezski North, Berezski Central and Berezski East. More than 30,000m had been drilled on the trend by July 2026, and 35 holes totalling ~24,000m were completed at Elemes in 2026 alone by mid-August.
  • Critically, Arras has no mineral resource estimate of any category on any property. The 5 May 2026 NI 43-101 technical report on Elemes, authored by independent QP Trevor Hart Wright (MSc, MAIG 4636, OreFinder Exploration), states that its resource, reserve, mining, processing, infrastructure, market, environmental, cost and economic sections are all 'not applicable to the current report', and that the author is unaware of any prior resource estimate at Elemes. The investment case is therefore purely discovery-stage.
What the Bozshakol-Chingiz belt offers
  • Elemes sits about 60 km southeast of KAZ Minerals' operating Bozshakol copper-gold mine, which reported Q1-2025 mined grades of 0.39% Cu and 0.19 g/t Au - a useful yardstick for what is economic in the district.
  • The Beskauga porphyry Cu-Au-Ag deposit lies roughly 80 km east of Elemes and carries a current NI 43-101 resource of 111.2 Mt @ 0.30% Cu, 0.49 g/t Au, 1.34 g/t Ag Indicated plus 92.6 Mt @ 0.24% Cu, 0.50 g/t Au, 1.14 g/t Ag Inferred. This deposit is NOT an Arras asset - see the Assets section - and is cited here only as belt endowment evidence.
  • Infrastructure is exceptional by exploration-company standards: a paved highway crosses the Elemes licence, 1100 KVA power lines and heavy rail are within a 15 km radius, and the Bogatyr thermal coal mine at Ekibastuz (~44 Mt/yr, supporting ~7.5 GW of generation and ~6,000 direct jobs) is 13 km away, sustaining a skilled regional mining workforce.
Geology and target types
  • The property is underlain by interbedded intermediate volcanic and sedimentary rocks intruded by multiple phases of diorite and monzodiorite porphyry. Copper-gold mineralisation occurs in sheeted and stockwork quartz-chalcopyrite veins associated with those intrusions.
  • A second, structurally controlled style is present: tourmaline-quartz-cemented breccias carrying patchy to disseminated pyrite-chalcopyrite with minor galena and sphalerite, tested by holes EL26035 and EL26037.
  • High-grade low-sulphidation epithermal veins have been mapped as a secondary target, with the K-Ozek epithermal target drilled in 2026. Property-wide soil sampling defined two extensive Cu-Mo-As anomalies - the Berezski and Aimandai trends - which frame the current drill priorities.
2Core Assets
  • Elemes (flagship, 100%): two licences, ~530 km2, three confirmed porphyry centres along ~10 km of the Berezski Trend. This is where essentially all 2026 capital is going, with roughly 50,000m of the ~60,000m company-wide 2026 drill target allocated here.
  • Berezski North is the scale story. EL26032 returned 935.9m @ 0.71% CuEq from surface (0.51 g/t Au, 0.19% Cu), including 214.9m @ 1.42% CuEq from 162.1m. August 2026 step-outs added EL26036 at 568.9m @ 0.33% CuEq from 78.0m (including 168.0m @ 0.51% CuEq) and EL26034 at 399.7m @ 0.26% CuEq from 390.3m (including 95.9m @ 0.47% CuEq). Earlier holes include 547m @ 0.73% CuEq (EL24005) and 52.4m @ 2.11% CuEq (EL25027).
  • Berezski Central has delivered 578m @ 0.39% CuEq (EL25012) and 261m @ 0.70% CuEq (EL24001). Berezski East - confirmed as the third porphyry centre in April 2026 - produced 115.7m @ 1.44 g/t Au & 0.24% Cu, plus 457m @ 0.63 g/t AuEq (EL25019) and 424m @ 0.50 g/t Au & 0.12% Cu (EL26031). Note the East centre is gold-dominant while North and Central are copper-equivalent-led.
  • Beskauga - important clarification: Arras no longer holds the Beskauga deposit. On 10 June 2025 the company elected not to exercise its purchase option and the option agreement with Copperbelt AG was mutually terminated, producing a US$3,619,303 impairment. Arras retains only the surrounding Beskauga West (2345-EL) and Beskauga East (2346-EL) exploration licences, eight blocks each. Any dataset still showing Arras as optionee over the Beskauga resource is out of date.
  • Regional licence portfolio (100%, self-funded since February 2026): Tay (2241-EL, 56 blocks), Karatol-1/-2/-3 (2608-EL / 2208-EL / 2367-EL), Azhe-1 (2207-EL, 58 blocks), 1866-EL (86 blocks), plus Ekidos, Stepnoe, Akkuduk, Norgubek and Maisor. Each block is about 2.1 km2. The former Teck Alliance ground reverted to Arras with roughly US$5M of partner-funded exploration data attached; 2026 plans there include MT and gravity at Shiderty, Bozshakol South and Besshoky with follow-up diamond drilling, ground magnetics at Tort Kuduk, and IP surveys at Akkuduk and Nogurbek.
  • Katwaro (Zambia) appears in the project database with Arras as optionor and Patriot at 100% - i.e. Arras is the grantor rather than the operator. It is not a core asset and carries no current work program in company disclosure; confirm any residual royalty or back-in rights directly with the company.

5Company Profile
  • Incorporated 5 February 2021 in British Columbia as part of an asset-purchase reorganisation of Silver Bull Resources, Inc. Head office: 1508-999 West Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC V6C 2W2. Investor relations: +1 604 687 5800, info@arrasminerals.com.
  • Listed on the TSX Venture Exchange as ARK and quoted on the OTCQB as ARRKF. All operations are conducted through wholly owned Kazakh subsidiary Ekidos Minerals LLP, acquired 3 February 2022.
  • Strategic positioning is deliberately first-mover: management's stated thesis is that Kazakhstan is emerging as a premium mining jurisdiction on prospective geology, existing infrastructure and a supportive permitting climate, and that Arras has assembled one of the country's largest copper-gold prospective land packages before that repricing is complete.
  • The macro backdrop the company points to includes the EU-Kazakhstan strategic partnership on critical raw materials, Germany's inaugural Germany-Central Asia summit, and major-company interest in the country - Barrick's stated intention to register locally and explore, and precedents such as a 35% interest in RG Gold behind a US$500M gold mine commissioned in 2023 producing roughly 180,000 oz Au per year.
  • Corporate governance documents including the Board of Directors Mandate are published on the website's governance page. Directors take part of their compensation in DSUs rather than cash. Arras retains 100% of the ground together with the partner-funded exploration dataset, but loses the associated management-fee income, which fell to US$14,148 in the six months from US$78,581 a year earlier. ↗ source
