1Overview
  • Southern Silver is a Canadian explorer-developer focused on the Cerro Las Minitas (CLM) silver-lead-zinc project in Durango, Mexico — one of the largest, highest-grade undeveloped silver deposits globally.
  • CLM hosts a 2024 MRE of 116 Moz AgEq Indicated + 186 Moz AgEq Inferred; a 2024 PEA returned after-tax NPV5% US$501M and 21.2% IRR. The Company also holds the Nazas and Puro Corazon projects in Mexico and the Oro Cu-Mo porphyry in New Mexico.
  • Shares trade TSX-V (SSV) and OTCQX (SSVFF); led by President Lawrence Page, a veteran precious-metals dealmaker with multiple mine discoveries to his record.
Strategic rationale
  • Tier-1 Faja de Plata silver belt in Durango with infrastructure and a skilled mining workforce.
  • Large, high-grade resource with robust PEA economics and no royalties.
  • Low valuation relative to asset scale, with several exploration growth levers.
2Core Assets
  • Cerro Las Minitas (100%): Ag-Pb-Zn skarn/CRD in Durango. 2024 MRE (effective Mar 20, 2024; US$60/t NSR cut-off): Indicated 13.30 Mt @ 101.6 g/t Ag, 3.14% Zn, 1.27% Pb, 0.07 g/t Au, 0.17% Cu (116 Moz AgEq); Inferred 23.4 Mt @ 110.7 g/t Ag, 2.14% Zn, 1.13% Pb, 0.14 g/t Au (186 Moz AgEq). 2024 PEA: 17-yr LOM, 5,300 t/d underground, after-tax NPV5% US$501M, IRR 21.2%, initial capital US$387.8M, ~14 Moz AgEq/yr; no royalties.
  • Nazas (option to earn 100%): greenfields Au-Ag-Pb-Zn project 12 km from Endeavour Silver's Pitarilla; drill-ready epithermal/porphyry targets; subject to a 2% NSR.
  • Puro Corazon claim: addition unlocking resource-growth and engineering efficiencies at CLM; underground channel sampling returned up to 2.8 m @ 757 g/t AgEq.
  • Oro (New Mexico, USA): Cu-Mo porphyry/skarn/CRD system on a major regional trend; early-stage. Minas de Ameca: exploration project in Jalisco, Mexico.
