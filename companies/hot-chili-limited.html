Investment highlights
  • Costa Fuego is one of a handful of globally significant, independent copper developments (not controlled by a major) capable of >100 ktpa CuEq supply, with a 2025 PFS NPV(8%) of US$1.2B, IRR 19% and 4.5-yr payback.
  • Large Indicated base of 798 Mt @ 0.45% CuEq and a maiden Probable Ore Reserve of 502 Mt @ 0.37% Cu — both reported to JORC/NI 43-101.
  • La Verde porphyry discovery (Dec 2024), 30 km from the plant, delivers wide near-surface high-grade hits (e.g. 472m @ 0.41% CuEq); a high-grade starter pit could lift production toward 150 ktpa CuEq and re-rate economics.
  • Rare ESG advantages: seawater processing in a freshwater-scarce region, existing Pan-American Highway / power / port access, no permanent camp needed.
  • Huasco Water (80% HCH / 20% CMP) holds the only granted maritime concession in the Huasco Valley — a strategic, multi-user water infrastructure business with staged NPV(8%) of US$122M (S1) / US$977M (S2).
Key risks
  • Pre-production developer: no current cash flow; relies on capital markets and the US$15M royalty for funding.
  • Chile permitting / EIA: the revised PFS and EIA submission (slated Q2 2027) remain key gating items.
  • Commodity price leverage: economics assume US$4.30/lb Cu / US$2,280/oz Au; spot Cu ~US$6.5/lb and Au ~US$4,300/oz are far higher but the project has not been re-priced.
  • La Verde remains pre-resource (maiden MRE expected late 2026); upside not yet in the mine plan.
  • Capital intensity: PFS capex ~US$1.27B; execution and financing risk for a project of this scale.

1Overview
  • Hot Chili (ASX/TSXV: HCH, OTCQX: HHLKF) is an emerging copper developer focused on the Costa Fuego Cu-Au project in coastal Atacama, Chile — one of few globally significant copper developments not controlled by a major mining house.
  • The 2025 Pre-Feasibility Study outlines a 20-year mine life (14-yr primary production) averaging ~116 kt CuEq/yr, with post-tax NPV(8%) of US$1.2B, IRR 19% and 4.5-yr payback at US$4.30/lb Cu / US$2,280/oz Au.
  • La Verde, a large Cu-Au porphyry discovered in December 2024, lies 30 km from the planned processing hub and is being advanced as a high-grade starter pit; a maiden MRE is targeted for late 2026.
  • Huasco Water is a strategic water-infrastructure business (80% HCH / 20% CMP) holding the only granted maritime concession in the Huasco Valley, supplying seawater to Costa Fuego and optionality for regional desalinated water.
  • Cross-check: PFS economics (NPV US$1.2B; capex US$1.27B; AISC US$1.38/lb Cu net of by-product credits) are consistent across the homepage, the July 2026 corporate presentation and the 24 Aug 2026 news commentary.

2Core Assets
  • Costa Fuego: three deposits (Productora, Cortadera, San Antonio; plus Alice) within a 10 km radius. Indicated Resource 798 Mt @ 0.45% CuEq (0.37% Cu, 0.1 g/t Au, 0.5 g/t Ag, 85 ppm Mo), effective 26 Feb 2024 [JORC / NI 43-101].
  • Costa Fuego Probable Ore Reserve 502 Mt @ 0.37% Cu, 0.1 g/t Au, 0.49 g/t Ag, 97 ppm Mo, effective 27 Mar 2025 [PFS NI 43-101]; based on Indicated blocks only.
  • Deposit styles: Cortadera = Cu-Au-Mo porphyry; San Antonio = lode-style skarn; Productora = tourmaline-breccia (IOCG/Manto-type); Alice = Cu-Mo porphyry.
  • La Verde: Cu-Au porphyry discovery (Dec 2024) with a 1,000m x 800m footprint open in all directions; step-out hits include 472m @ 0.41% CuEq from 49m (incl. 105.8m @ 0.70% CuEq). Maiden MRE late 2026; could lift production toward 150 ktpa CuEq.
  • Huasco Water: 3-stage seawater/desalination network. Stage 1 NPV(8%) US$122M (IRR 19%, capex US$151M); Stage 2 (1,300 L/s) NPV(8%) US$977M (IRR 19%, capex US$1.4B).
  • Cross-check: CR cites '3.62 Mt contained CuEq' Indicated (0.45% x 798 Mt ≈ 3.59 Mt) and '640 kt CuEq' inferred — reconciling with the MRE table; figures are internally consistent.
