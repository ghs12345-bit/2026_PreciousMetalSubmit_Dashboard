Investment highlights
  • Green Bay Copper-Gold PEA (2026) outlines a high-grade, low-cost, long-life operation: post-tax NPV7% >A$3.0B (4.6 Mtpa) / >A$2.2B (1.8 Mtpa) and ~40-42% IRR, peaking >100 kt CuEq/yr.
  • Large, growing resource: 60.2 Mt M&I @ 2.43% CuEq (1,464 Kt CuEq) plus 23.5 Mt Inferred @ 2.51% (592 Kt); Ming high-grade core 18.1 Mt @ 4.3% CuEq.
  • Low-capital brownfield path: existing Ming decline to 950 m, 650 m shaft, Nugget Pond mill (500 ktpa) and Pine Cove port access leverage fast-track development.
  • Strong treasury (~A$230M cash at Dec 2025) funds studies and early works.
  • District-scale 346 km2 tenure in the prolific Baie Verte VMS belt (Little Deer, Tilt Cove, Rambler) for organic growth.
Key risks
  • Only a PEA to date — outcomes are preliminary and may not be realized; requires FS, permits and financing.
  • Single advanced project (Green Bay/Ming) in Newfoundland; execution, ramp-up and infrastructure (power, tailings) risk.
  • Commodity-price exposure to assumed US$10,626/t Cu; A$/US$ FX risk.
  • Historical narrow-VMS mining style; reserve conversion and grade reconciliation risk.
  • Permitting/community considerations in Newfoundland; reliance on partner port access.
Near-term catalysts
  • Feasibility study progressing post-PEA; optimization of the 4.6 Mtpa case.
  • Ongoing resource-growth drilling at Ming (high-grade core open at depth; deepest hole 49 m @ 6.1% CuEq).
  • Regional exploration across 346 km2 tenure (Little Deer, Tilt Cove).
  • Potential project financing / strategic-partner discussions.
  • Permitting and early-works advancement toward a development decision.
