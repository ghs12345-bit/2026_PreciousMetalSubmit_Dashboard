Investment highlights
  • One of the world's top silver producers: 2025 output of ~17.6 Moz silver, 184,000 t zinc and 50,000 t lead from a 52,000 tpd concentrator in southwestern Bolivia.
  • Large-scale, long-life open-pit Ag-Zn-Pb operation with established infrastructure and a track record of consistent, low-cost base- and precious-metal production.
  • 'Future Growth' expansion initiatives referenced on the corporate site signal potential to extend mine life and/or increase throughput.
  • Strong ESG and transparency posture for a private miner: publishes annual ESTMA (Extractive Sector Transparency Measures Act) reports and Modern Slavery Reports.
  • Privately held with corporate-owner financial strength and operational expertise; not exposed to equity-market financing risk.
Key risks
  • Commodity-price exposure: revenue is driven by silver, zinc and lead prices, all of which are volatile.
  • Bolivia jurisdiction risk: taxation, royalty and policy changes, plus social/community and permitting considerations for any expansion.
  • Private-company opacity: no public financial statements, resource/reserve disclosures or shareholder structure — limited external visibility.
  • Operating risks of a large high-altitude open-pit operation: processing, water/energy, weather and geotechnical factors.
  • Expansion execution risk: any growth project requires capital, permits and community consent and is not guaranteed.
Near-term catalysts
  • Advancement of the 'Future Growth' expansion program to extend mine life / increase throughput.
  • Continued strong silver production amid supportive precious-metals prices.
  • Potential resource/reserve updates or new public disclosures as the operation matures.

1Overview
  • San Cristobal Mining operates the San Cristobal mine, a large open-pit silver-zinc-lead operation in Potosi Department, southwestern Bolivia — among the largest and lowest-cost Ag-Zn-Pb mines globally.
  • 2025 production of ~17.6 Moz silver, 184,000 t zinc and 50,000 t lead from a 52,000 tpd concentrator; the operation ranks among the world's top silver producers.
  • A private company: equity is not publicly listed, so there is no ticker and no public financial filings beyond ESTMA and Modern Slavery reports.
Operating profile
  • Single, integrated mining-concentrator complex recovering silver, zinc and lead.
  • Long-life, low-cost producer in a proven mining district.
  • Emphasis on sustainability and transparent extractive-sector reporting.
