1Overview
  • South Pacific Metals (TSX-V: SPMC) is a Canadian explorer with a district-scale gold-copper portfolio of four projects (Osena, Anga, Kili Teke, May River) spanning >3,100 km2 in Papua New Guinea's Papuan Fold & Thrust Belt.
  • Two complementary pillars: an established NI 43-101 Inferred resource of 4.2 Moz AuEq at Kili Teke, and a high-potential discovery search at Osena/Ontenu immediately southwest of K92 Mining's Kainantu mine.
  • A December 2025 BMO-brokered financing funds an active two-rig drill campaign; shares also trade OTCQB (SPMEF) and FSE (6J00).
Strategic rationale
  • Same belt as world-class PNG mines (Porgera, Kainantu, Frieda River, Wafi-Golpu).
  • Kili Teke provides resource scale; Osena offers near-term discovery upside next to a producing mine.
  • Funded, with a team that has built and financed major gold companies.
2Core Assets
  • Kili Teke (100%): Cu-Au porphyry in Hela Province; NI 43-101 Inferred Resource (Graeme Fleming, effective 2021; report Nov 18, 2022) of 237 Mt @ 0.34% Cu, 0.24 g/t Au (~168 ppm Mo) = 802 kt Cu, 1.81 Moz Au, 40 kt Mo (4.2 Moz AuEq / 3.14B lbs CuEq). The highest grades (skarns: 7.8 m @ 12.98% Cu, 11.75 g/t Au) lie outside the resource; 14 machine-learning-ranked targets (6 Rank-1). Field program commenced Jun 2026.
  • Osena (100%): 738 km2 on the Kainantu Transfer Zone, immediately SW of K92's Kainantu mine. Flagship Ontenu prospect: maiden drilling (7 holes, 2,266 m; results May 2026) cut 92 m of mineralization at Ontenu NE (peak 9.92 g/t Au, 2.35% Cu), confirming a K92-style intermediate-sulphidation epithermal system; 8 mapped NW-trending structures, most undrilled. Ontenu Central shows a 2 km shallow porphyry Cu-Au target.
  • Anga: additional Cu-Au project on the Kainantu Transfer Zone (~1,300 km2 combined with Osena).
  • May River: early-stage VMS/copper-gold target (historic Tuwavi VMS); dataset reprocessing underway.
