1Overview
  • Magna Mining Inc. (TSX: NICU; OTCQX: MGMNF; FSE: 8YD) is a Canadian base- and precious-metals producer focused on the Sudbury Basin, Ontario, with copper, nickel and PGM (platinum, palladium, gold, silver) assets.
  • The Company's primary asset is the producing McCreedy West copper-PGE-nickel mine, supported by a pipeline of past-producing properties: Levack, Crean Hill, Podolsky and Shakespeare.
  • Strategy pairs cash generation from McCreedy West with advancement of a development pipeline toward multiple future production centres in the Sudbury district.
Operating model
  • McCreedy West ore is processed by Vale at the Clarabelle Mill under an offtake agreement.
  • Q2 2026 set records for tonnage and contained CuEq; cash costs US$3.76/CuEq lb, AISC US$4.54/CuEq lb, production cost $199/ton (-6.9% QoQ).
2Core Assets
  • McCreedy West (Sudbury): operating Cu-PGE-Ni mine. Indicated resource 5,632,000 t @ 1.10% Cu, 0.98% Ni, 0.82 g/t Pt, 0.92 g/t Pd, 0.23 g/t Au; Inferred 874,000 t @ 1.37% Cu, 1.00% Ni. Probable reserve 987,000 t @ 1.59% Cu, 0.32% Ni (eff. Dec 31, 2025).
  • Shakespeare (70km SW Sudbury): feasibility-stage, permitted 4,500 tpd Ni-Cu-PGM project. Total Indicated 20.34 Mt @ 0.55% NiEq; Inferred 2.36 Mt @ 0.57% NiEq; Probable reserve (open pit) 11.87 Mt (Jan 2022).
  • Crean Hill: brownfield past-producer with NI 43-101 resource >30 Mt, including 14.5 Mt high-grade underground (SGS 2022); PFS on track Q3 2026.
  • Levack: advanced past-producer with historical high-grade Footwall production (Morrison: 2.0 Mt @ 7.1% Cu, 1.4% Ni, 8.1 g/t PGM); new technical report filed Dec 31, 2025; PEA targeted Q3 2026. R2 Footwall Zone returned 29.7% CuEq over 3.4m (Jun 2026).
  • Podolsky: permitted past-producing Ni-Cu-PGM project in the Sudbury basin.
