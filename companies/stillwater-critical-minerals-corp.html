1Overview
  • Stillwater Critical Minerals (TSX-V: PGE) is an exploration/development company advancing the 100%-owned Stillwater West Ni-PGE-Cu-Co-Cr + Au project in Montana's Stillwater mining district — directly adjacent to Sibanye-Stillwater's producing PGE mine, the only primary PGE producer in the United States.
  • Stillwater West is one of the largest undeveloped polymetallic critical-mineral systems in the U.S.: a Jan 2023 NI 43-101 MRE of 255 Mt @ 0.39% NiEq (1.19 g/t PdEq), 1.6 Blbs Ni+Cu+Co, 3.8 Moz 4E (Pd+Pt+Rh+Au) and 2.3 Blbs Cr; an updated 2026 MRE is targeted for H1 2026.
  • The Company is backed by strategic investor Glencore (~13–15%) and a technical team with Bushveld/Platreef experience; it also holds the Kluane PGE-Ni-Cu project (Yukon), a 49% interest in Drayton-Black Lake gold (Ontario) and the Duke Island Cu-Ni-PGE project (Alaska).
Strategic rationale
  • Tier-1 U.S. jurisdiction beside an operating mine with infrastructure, power, rail and skilled labour; FAST-41 federal priority.
  • Hosts 10 U.S.-designated critical minerals (Ni, Cu, Co, Pt, Pd, Rh, Ir, Ru, Cr, Te) — direct strategic supply relevance.
  • District-scale, open-ended system with resource-growth and higher-grade upside; validated by Glencore.

  • Kluane (Spy, Ultra, Catalyst): Yukon PGE-Ni-Cu project on trend with the Wellgreen/Nickel Shaw deposit; grassroots-exploration stage with high-grade historic grab samples (e.g., Spy 90.7 g/t 3E, 10.5% Cu).
  • Drayton-Black Lake: NW Ontario gold project (142 km2) adjacent to NexGold's Goliath complex; 49% interest / Heritage Mining may earn up to 90% via option.
  • Duke Island: SE Alaska Cu-Ni-PGE project (Alexander Platinum Belt); early-stage with surface samples up to 1.95% Cu, 0.25% Ni and 1 g/t PGE. Yankee-Dundee (BC): past-producing Ag-Au camp, retained back-in/royalty following the 2013 sale.
