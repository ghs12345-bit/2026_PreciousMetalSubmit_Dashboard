Key risks
  • No economic study of any kind exists: the sole NI 43-101 resource is a 2023 mineral resource estimate with no reserves, no PEA, PFS or feasibility study, and therefore no published NPV, IRR, capex or production profile — value rests entirely on exploration outcomes.
  • Resource staleness and narrow coverage: the MCZ estimate has an effective date of 1 May 2023, roughly three years old, excludes all 2024–2026 drilling, and covers only one of 15 identified Peruvian skarn targets; metallurgy remains at preliminary flotation and leach test stage only.
  • Going-concern language and perpetual dilution: financial statements disclose material uncertainties casting significant doubt on going concern, and the company has funded itself through successive equity raises at C$0.30 (Oct 2024), C$0.50 (Mar 2025) and C$1.10 (Feb 2026), taking shares from 61.9M to 125.3M in under two years.
  • Concentrated jurisdictional and physical exposure: C$49.3M of exploration assets sit in Peru — where community agreements (e.g. the pending Yanque community accord required for the Annie I target) can gate access — and C$24.8M in Jamaica, where Hurricane Melissa halted Bellas Gate and Super Block work for roughly eight weeks after landfall on 28 October 2025.
  • Royalty and deferred-payment overhang on the best assets: Jasperoide carries a 2% Ares NSR (1% buyable for US$1M) plus a prospective 1.5% La Bruja NSR with US$770k of option payments still due, while Bellas Gate / Browns Hall owe OZ Minerals (now BHP) C$8.5M and C$4M after commercial production plus a 2% NSR capped at C$20M, and a CCM NSR being stepped down to 1% through payments running to a US$500,000 fifth-anniversary instalment.

1Overview
  • C3 Metals Inc. (TSX-V: CCCM; OTCQB: CUAUF) is a junior copper-gold exploration company incorporated under the Business Corporations Act (Ontario) on 29 March 2010, with its registered office and corporate function at 69 Yonge Street, Suite 200, Toronto, while all material exploration assets sit in southern Peru and Jamaica.
  • The strategy is explicitly portfolio-driven: build district-scale copper-gold land packages, evaluate them systematically, then at a data-driven decision point either keep 100% (good near-surface grade, low capital intensity), partner (large but capital-intensive or synergistic with a neighbour), or divest and recycle capital.
  • Peru anchors the value: the 30,599-hectare Jasperoide project in the Andahuaylas-Yauri porphyry-skarn belt sits in the same geological setting as Las Bambas (MMG), Constancia (Hudbay) and Antapaccay (Glencore), and hosts the company's only NI 43-101 resource at Montana de Cobre plus 14 further undrilled skarn targets and two porphyry targets across two parallel 28km belts.
  • Jamaica provides the partner-funded upside: C3 holds 100% of 17,855 hectares and 50% of a further 9,870 hectares, with 13,020 hectares of the wholly-owned ground inside the Freeport-McMoRan earn-in, and has mapped 16 porphyry, 40 epithermal and multiple volcanic redbed prospects over a 30km strike length.
  • At 31 May 2026 the balance sheet carried C$74.07M of capitalised exploration and evaluation assets against C$1.11M of total liabilities and C$27.02M of cash, with shareholders' equity of C$100.56M and a nine-month net loss of only C$2.52M — a low-burn, asset-heavy explorer structure.
Corporate structure
  • Wholly-owned subsidiaries: Carube Resources Inc. (Canada, holding), Carube Resources Jamaica Limited (exploration), Rodinia Jamaica Limited (exploration), Latin America Resource Group Limited (Canada, holding), C3 Metals Peru S.A.C. (exploration) and Molino Azul S.A.C. (exploration).
  • GP C3 JV Limited is held 50% and exists to hold the Super Block SEPLs in trust for C3 and Geophysx Jamaica Ltd.
  • C3 Metals was formed in 2020 through the merger of Latin America Resource Group — founded by director Kimberly Ann in 2017 and which assembled Jasperoide from two small concessions — with Carube Copper Corp.

Jurisdictional context
  • Peru is the third-largest copper producer worldwide at roughly 11% of global output and about 10% of national GDP, producing 2.2 Mt in 2022, with Cerro Verde in operation since the 1970s.
  • Jamaica has had mining legislation in place since 1947, mining is the second-largest industry, bauxite exports run US$500–600M annually, and the company reports two to three months to obtain five-year drill permits.
  • Barrick Gold entered an earn-in with Geophysx Jamaica in May 2024 over approximately 400,000 hectares, several blocks of which surround C3's concessions — a second major validating the district.
2Core Assets
  • Five projects carry capitalised exploration value at 31 May 2026: Jasperoide C$38.39M, Bellas Gate C$21.54M, Khaleesi C$10.91M, Super Block C$2.68M and Hungry Gully C$0.56M, totalling C$74.07M.
  • Khaleesi absorbed the largest incremental spend in the nine months to May 2026 at roughly C$8.47M (including C$3.64M drilling and C$2.63M geology and field costs), reflecting the shift of capital toward the newest discovery.
  • Montana de Cobre (MCZ), within Jasperoide, is the only systematically drill-tested target of 13–15 identified skarn prospects and the only asset with an NI 43-101 mineral resource: 51.9 Mt @ 0.50% Cu and 0.20 g/t Au M&I for 569.1 M lbs copper and 326,800 oz gold, plus 4.0 Mt @ 0.32% Cu and 0.11 g/t Au inferred.
  • The MCZ deposit is a shallow-dipping copper-gold skarn oxidised to more than 200m vertical depth with 50–250m true thickness, amenable to open-pit mining at low-to-moderate strip ratio, and includes a high-grade core of 15.9 Mt @ 1.1% Cu and 0.35 g/t Au at a 0.45% Cu cut-off.
  • Bellas Gate hosts three drill-confirmed porphyry systems — Connors, Camel Hill and Provost — with historical intercepts including 309.0m @ 0.44% Cu and 0.33 g/t Au, 79.0m @ 0.71% Cu and 0.49 g/t Au (incl. 46.0m @ 0.95% Cu), and 285.9m @ 0.37% Cu and 0.21 g/t Au; many holes ended in mineralisation, motivating the 2026–27 deep program.
  • Super Block combines Geophysx's licences over the past-producing Pennants gold mine with C3's Main Ridge SEPL and part of Arthurs Seat; Pennants produced a reported 16,000 oz gold in 2002–2004 before closing, with a non-compliant historical estimate of 75,500t @ 20.4 g/t Au that must not be relied upon.
  • Three Canadian occurrences appear in third-party mine databases — Fiedmont (Quebec, Pt-Pd), Salal (BC, Mo) and White Cross (BC, Cu) — but the FY2026 segment note shows nil Canadian exploration and evaluation asset value, so these should be treated as legacy or non-material holdings pending confirmation.

5Company Profile
  • C3 Metals' stated mission is to discover and delineate meaningful copper-gold deposits to help supply copper for the global clean-energy transition, on the view that a structural copper deficit will emerge over the coming decades.
  • The company positions itself as a first mover in Jamaica, having taken ground before Geophysx consolidated the island's licences and before Barrick's May 2024 earn-in with Geophysx over roughly 400,000 hectares.
  • In Peru the thesis is downside protection plus blue sky: an existing near-surface oxide-dominated resource at Montana de Cobre provides a valuation floor, while 14 untested skarns and two porphyry targets across two 28km belts, and the emerging Khaleesi discovery, provide the upside.
  • Analyst coverage comes from ATB Cormark Capital Markets (Richard Gray) and Paradigm Capital (Lauren McConnell); the company lists 14 institutional holders including Resource Capital Funds, Equinox Partners, Ninepoint Partners, Konwave AG, Donald Smith & Company and CQS.
  • Note on data sourced from history: Jasperoide information partly derives from Hochschild's database from two drill programs in 2011–2012, and the company states it has not completed sufficient work to verify all historic information; Pennants historical figures are expressly non-compliant with NI 43-101.
