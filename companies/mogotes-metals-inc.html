1Overview
  • Mogotes Metals Inc. (TSX-V: MOG; FSE: OY4; OTCQB: MOGMF) is a copper-gold-silver porphyry explorer advancing three projects across three continents: Filo Sur (Argentina/Chile), Beskauga (Kazakhstan) and Copper Cliff (Montana, USA).
  • The flagship Filo Sur lies in the prolific Vicuña District, directly adjacent to BHP and Lundin Mining's Filo del Sol Cu-Au-Ag development project.
  • The Company combines a discovery-stage flagship with a resource-stage asset (Beskauga) and a major-JV opportunity (Copper Cliff, under option from Rio Tinto).
Strategy
  • Tier-one porphyry potential via district-scale land in premier copper jurisdictions.
  • Counter-cyclical Beskauga (Central Asia) and Filo Sur (South America) seasons provide continuous exploration.
2Core Assets
  • Filo Sur (Argentina/Chile, Vicuña District): 100%-optioned Cu-Au-Ag porphyry directly adjacent to Filo del Sol. The 2026 season returned 'discovery' intercepts at the Arbor and Cruz del Sur (CDS) targets, located along strike from the BHP/Lundin system; early-stage, no resource yet.
  • Beskauga (Kazakhstan): resource-stage Cu-Au-Ag porphyry. Historical 2022 NI 43-101 MRE (GMV cut-off US$20/t): Indicated 111.2 Mt @ 0.30% Cu, 0.49 g/t Au, 1.34 g/t Ag (333.6 kt Cu, 1.8 Moz Au, 4.8 Moz Ag) and Inferred 92.6 Mt @ 0.24% Cu, 0.50 g/t Au, 1.14 g/t Ag (222.2 kt Cu, 1.5 Moz Au, 3.4 Moz Ag). Higher-grade core starts ~40m below surface; post-MRE drilling (18,657 m) extended mineralization (e.g. BG21007: 1,124m @ 0.40 g/t Au, 0.25% Cu, 1.69 g/t Ag from 46m).
  • Copper Cliff (Montana, USA): gold-copper porphyry optioned from Rio Tinto's Kennecott; Mogotes can earn up to 60% by sole-funding staged exploration. Historic district with a defined exploration target.
