1Overview
  • Element 29 is a Peru-focused junior explorer developing the Elida porphyry Cu-Mo-Ag deposit, with a pipeline of three additional early-stage porphyry Cu prospects (Flor de Cobre, Paka, Pahuay) on 25,000+ ha of titled concessions.
  • Management and board comprise porphyry Cu discovery veterans (ex-Teck, Anglo American, Lundin) with track records at Zafranal and adjacent to Antamina.
  • Strategy: grow Elida's resource while retaining optionality on new discoveries; Cu and Mo are recognized critical minerals for the energy transition.
Investment thesis
  • Large, near-surface Inferred resource with expansion runway
  • Strong strategic/shareholder base (Acasta Partners, Resource Capital Fund)
  • Low-cost Peru jurisdiction with existing infrastructure
2Core Assets
  • Four porphyry Cu projects in Peru; Elida is the advanced flagship with an NI 43-101 (Sept 20, 2022) inferred MRE.
  • Elida: 321.7 Mt @ 0.32% Cu, 0.029% Mo, 2.6 g/t Ag at 0.20% Cu cutoff, strip ratio 0.74:1; higher-grade starter subset 59.7 Mt @ 0.49% Cu, 0.036% Mo, 3.99 g/t Ag (0.40% Cu cutoff). [authoritative NI 43-101, pending update]
  • Flor de Cobre: prospect in the Southern Peru Copper Belt, 26 km from Cerro Verde (Freeport); Paka and Pahuay are skarn/porphyry prospects with surface samples up to 4-6.5% Cu.
  • Cross-check: corporate-presentation headlines are consistent with the NI 43-101 figures; no material discrepancy in the headline resource.
