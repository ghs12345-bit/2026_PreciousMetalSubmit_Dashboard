1Overview
  • Blue Moon Metals is a Canadian-listed (TSX-V: MOON; NASDAQ: BMM) mineral exploration & development company focused on polymetallic brownfield projects in Tier-1 jurisdictions.
  • Flagship is the Nussir Cu-Au-Ag project in Finnmark, Norway (94.52%-owned Nussir ASA), now at Feasibility + FID with production targeted 2027.
  • Since 2025 the company rapidly built a US critical-minerals hub-and-spoke platform (Blue Moon Zn project CA; Springer tungsten NV; Apex Ge/Ga UT; 33-project tungsten/antimony portfolio).
Business model
  • Nussir is the near-term cash-flow engine (Cu-Au-Ag, EU Strategic Project designation).
  • US assets are leveraged through the Springer processing complex (hub-and-spoke) for tungsten, germanium, gallium and antimony.
Asset ownership
  • Nussir ASA 94.52% (Norway Cu-Au-Ag); NSG / Sulitjelma 100% (Norway Cu-Zn exploration).
  • Keystone Mines 100% (Blue Moon, California Zn-Cu-Au-Ag).
  • Springer (Nevada) 100%; Apex (Utah) 100% from Teck (Mar-2026); Gage (UT) acquired Apr-2026; 33-project US portfolio (Aug-2026, pending).
