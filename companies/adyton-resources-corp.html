•Project Map
1Overview
  • Adyton is a PNG-focused gold-copper explorer/developer (TSX-V: ADY) holding two island projects: Feni (New Ireland) and Fergusson (Milne Bay).
  • Total NI 43-101 resource is 2.52 Moz Au; the company positions Fergusson for near-term cash flow and Feni for district-scale discovery.
  • Treasury is preserved: JV partner EVIH funds Fergusson to production at zero Adyton capital outlay, while Adyton retains 50% of free cash flow.
Strategic rationale
  • Dual-track: a de-risked near-term production pillar (Fergusson) plus a high-upside exploration pillar (Feni).
  • Coastal/island logistics lower capex vs PNG highland projects (pit-to-ship ~5 km at Feni vs ~1,350 km at Ok Tedi).
2Core Assets
  • Feni Island (100% Adyton): flagship growth asset, 1.46 Moz Au Inferred at Kabang (Oct 2021 43-101). Alkalic epithermal-porphyry in the Lihir trend; Cu is not in the MRE but historical holes show up to +56% AuEq uplift where Cu was assayed.
  • Fergusson Island (50/50 JV with EVIH): 1.067 Moz Au (Indicated 441 + Inferred 626 koz). Wapolu (shallow, historic producer, restart targeted H2 2026) plus Gameta (611 koz, Indicated +131% in Aug 2026).
