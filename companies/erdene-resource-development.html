Investment highlights
  • High-grade, low-cost gold producer: Bayan Khundii achieved commercial production in Q1 2026 — one of the highest-grade open-pit gold mines globally (reserves 513,700 oz @ 4.0 g/t; ~85,000 oz/yr).
  • Prolific Khundii Minerals District in SW Mongolia with multiple deposits (Bayan Khundii, Altan Nar, Dark Horse, Zuun Mod Mo-Cu) offering district-scale growth.
  • Q2 2026 the JV sold 11,709 oz gold at US$4,493/oz; quarterly feed grade 2.4 g/t, production ramping after first gold (Q3 2025).
  • Strong exploration upside: near-surface high-grade intercepts outside the modelled orebody and a 20 km² Dark Horse prospect area.
  • Strategic JV with Mongolian Mining Corporation (MMC); gold sold to Mongolia's Central Bank, supporting national FX reserves.
Key risks
  • Single-mine producer in early production ramp-up; throughput and grade reconciliation risk.
  • Jurisdiction risk: Mongolia permitting, royalty/profit-share regime and political considerations.
  • 1% NSR to Sandstorm Gold plus first-400,000-oz bonus clauses dilute mine economics.
  • Remote SW Mongolia logistics; reliance on MMC JV partner and Central Bank offtake.
  • Gold price and FX exposure; limited operating history at nameplate scale.
Near-term catalysts
  • Continued production ramp and grade/throughput improvements at Bayan Khundii through 2026.
  • Resource-expansion drilling at Bayan Khundii / Dark Horse extending mine life.
  • Advancement of Altan Nar (Au-Ag-Pb-Zn) and Zuun Mod (Mo-Cu) toward development.
  • New district discoveries along the Trans Altai Terrane.
  • Potential restructuring/monetization of the MMC JV or additional strategic funding.

1Overview
  • Erdene is a Mongolia-focused gold producer and explorer that transitioned from greenfield discovery to production in ~10 years, with first gold at Bayan Khundii in Q3 2025 and commercial production in Q1 2026.
  • Flagship Bayan Khundii is a near-surface, high-grade open-pit mine in the Khundii Minerals District; the Company is expanding resources and exploring satellite/deposit upside.
  • Strategy: build a long-life gold district while harvesting cash flow from Bayan Khundii to fund organic exploration.
