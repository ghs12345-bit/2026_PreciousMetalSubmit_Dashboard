1Overview
  • Meridian Mining plc (LSE/TSX/OTC: MNO) is a Brazil-focused mineral development company advancing the Cabaçal copper-gold-silver VMS project in Mato Grosso, Brazil.
  • Cabaçal is an advanced-stage, shallow, open-pittable VMS system with a 2025 PFS and a Definitive Feasibility Study targeted for Q4 2026.
  • The Company also holds earlier-stage exploration ground across a ~50 km VMS belt and additional Brazilian and international assets.
Development plan
  • 2025 PFS: NPV(5%) US$984M, IRR 61.2%, initial capex US$248M, ~141,000 oz AuEq/yr, ~17-month payback.
  • Following LSE admission (May 2026), Meridian is ordering long-lead items and advancing pre-construction works at Cabaçal.
2Core Assets
  • Cabaçal (Mato Grosso): Cu-Au-Ag VMS. M&I resource (effective Dec 31, 2025) 70.10 Mt @ 0.56 g/t Au, 1.31 g/t Ag, 0.33% Cu — contained 1,259 koz Au, 2,960 koz Ag, 233 kt Cu. Reserve statement (Feb 2025): 89% Proven category.
  • 2025 PFS economics: after-tax NPV(5%) US$984M; IRR 61.2%; initial capex US$248M; payback ~17 months; average ~141,000 oz AuEq/yr; base case Au US$2,119/oz, Cu US$4.16/lb.
  • Broader Cabaçal VMS belt (~50 km) under Meridian control offers resource-expansion and district-scale upside; exploration ongoing.
  • Additional assets include earlier-stage exploration projects (e.g. Espigão, Santa Helena) in Brazil and legacy international interests.
