Key risks
  • Pre-production explorer-developer with no revenue; value hinges on resource growth, a PEA/PFS, permitting and eventual project financing for a very large capital project.
  • Jurisdiction / social-license risk in Peru: although 10 community agreements are in place at Cotabambas, permitting and community consent for a major mine remain execution-critical amid historical regional unrest.
  • Dilution risk: ongoing financings (C$21.0M LIFE offering; 4.825M options granted Jul 2026 at C$1.70) lift the share count to 284.3M (Mar 31, 2026) and may continue.
  • Balance-sheet pressure: accumulated deficit $47.86M and working-capital deficiency $17.32M as of Mar 31, 2026; Q1 FY2026 loss $3.48M — reliance on continuous raises.
  • Resource-stage uncertainty: resources are M&I/Inferred (not reserves); CuEq at a 0.15% cut-off with recovery assumptions; Antilla stake diluted to 25% (royalty only).

1Overview
  • Panoro is a Canadian copper exploration and development company focused on large-scale Cu-Au-Ag-Mo porphyry projects in southern Peru's Andahuaylas-Yauri batholith — the belt hosting Las Bambas, Constancia, Antapaccay and Quellaveco.
  • Flagship is the 100%-owned Cotabambas project (Apurimac); secondary interests include a 25% retained stake plus 2% NSR in the Antilla Cu project (75% sold in 2021), the Humamantata JV (JOGMEC), and a portfolio of early-stage prospects.
  • Shares trade on TSX-V (PML), BVL (PML), FSE (PZM) and OTCQB (POROF); led by CEO Luquman Shaheen since 2008, with strategic partners Wheaton Precious Metals, Hudbay and JOGMEC.
Strategic rationale
  • Tier-1 district with established infrastructure (power, roads, ports) and multiple world-class operating mines — de-risks eventual development.
  • Two advanced-stage projects (Cotabambas, Antilla) carry resources + PEAs; Cotabambas Indicated resource grew 333% in the 2024 update.
  • Financing partnerships (Wheaton streaming, Hudbay/Kusiorcco sale, JOGMEC JV) reduce dilution versus pure equity raises.
