  • Jurisdiction (Chile/Argentina) permitting, water/community and FX exposure; early-stage grade/continuity unproven.

2Core Assets
  • Buen Retiro (Chile, IOCG): 13,840 ha; historical Manto Negro oxide mine; ~26,000 m of recent drilling; main trend extended to 1.9 km; resource definition + PFS (heap leach) underway, target Q2 2027. No published MRE yet [NI 43-101 TR Feb 2025 confirms no current estimate].
  • Caballos (Chile, Cu-Mo-Au): 18,000 ha; 10+ km chargeable trend along the Pocuro Fault; 2026 IP identified large anomalies linked to Cu-Mo-Au intercepts; NI 43-101 (Jul 2024) confirms no current resource estimate.
  • Polimet (Chile, Au-Cu-Ag epithermal veins): high-grade historical production (DSO 33.5 g/t Au, 6.9% Cu); early-stage.
  • Taquetren (Argentina, Ag) and diamond projects (Courageous Lake, Hearne Lake, Kolandoto) provide commodity diversification.
  • Cross-check: technical reports for both Caballos and Buen Retiro explicitly state 'no historical or current mineral resource estimates' — headline resource figures are not yet available; deck claims are exploration targets only.
