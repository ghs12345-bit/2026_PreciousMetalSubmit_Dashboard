  • Carangas PEA delivers exceptional economics — post-tax NPV(5%) US$2.65B and 35.9% IRR at base case, rising to US$4.16B NPV at US$67.50/oz Ag.
  • Silver Sand PFS outlines a 13-year, 12 Moz/yr silver operation with low AISC of US$10.69/oz and initial capex of only US$358M.

Key risks
  • Pre-production developer with no revenue; value depends on permitting, financing and construction of very large projects.
  • Jurisdiction risk: Bolivia — state holds ~1.85% of Carangas mineral resource in the southern pit; tenure and community/state approvals are critical.
  • Carangas PEA relies on Inferred resources and a deeper gold zone requiring state-held ground; PEA outcomes are preliminary.
  • Silver/gold price leverage; NPV highly sensitive (US$973M at US$22.50/oz Ag vs US$4.16B at US$67.50/oz).
  • Dilution risk as projects advance to Feasibility and construction financing.
Near-term catalysts

  • 30,000 m infill + exploration drilling campaign at Carangas commencing September 2026.
  • Commencement of Environmental Impact Assessment (EEIA) and Feasibility Study for Carangas once AMCs granted.
  • Continued progress on Silver Sand permitting toward a future construction decision.

1Overview
  • New Pacific is a Canadian exploration and development company (Vancouver HQ) advancing two permitting-stage precious-metals projects and one exploration project, all in Bolivia.
  • Flagship Silver Sand (Potosí) has potential to become one of the world's largest silver mines; Carangas (Oruro) is a large silver-gold system; Silverstrike (La Paz) is early-stage.
  • Shares trade on NYSE American (NEWP) and TSX (NUAG). Over a decade of operating experience in Bolivia.
Strategic rationale
  • First-mover in an underexplored, silver-endowed jurisdiction (Bolivia is the 4th-largest silver producer).
  • Two complementary assets spanning silver-only (Silver Sand) and silver-gold polymetallic (Carangas) exposure.
  • Experienced board with Silvercorp/Pan American/Zijin linkages and deep Bolivia operating relationships.
2Core Assets
