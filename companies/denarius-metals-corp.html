1Overview
  • Denarius Metals is a Canadian (BC) junior engaged in the acquisition, exploration, development and operation of polymetallic projects in Spain and Colombia; listed on Cboe Canada (DMET) and OTCQX (DNRSF).
  • An emerging metals producer: Zancudo (Colombia) is in early gold-silver production with a 1,000 tpd plant due in Q4-2026, while Aguablanca (Spain) is targeted to restart within 12 months.
  • Four-pillar portfolio: 100% Zancudo (Au-Ag), 21.8% Aguablanca (Ni-Cu-PGM), 100% Lomero (Cu-Zn-Pb-Au-Ag VMS) and 100% Toral (Zn-Pb-Ag) — plus a 15.34% strategic stake in Copper Giant (Mocoa Cu-Mo).
  • Q2-2026 revenue reached US$4.9M (H1 US$8.4M), up sharply from US$1.7M in full-year 2025, as Zancudo early production ramps; a US$28.8M Trafigura financing funded a Copper Giant investment.
