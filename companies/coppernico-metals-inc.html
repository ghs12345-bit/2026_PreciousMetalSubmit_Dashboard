1Overview
  • Coppernico Metals Inc. is a Vancouver-based (BC) mineral exploration company focused on the discovery of large-scale, high-grade copper-gold deposits in the Americas; TSX: COPR since 2024.
  • Flagship asset is the ~57,000 ha Sombrero copper-gold project in southern Peru (Ayacucho), on the northwestern extension of the prolific Andahuaylas-Yauri belt that hosts Las Bambas, Tintaya and Antapaccay.
  • The project comprises a district-scale cluster of Cu-Au skarn, porphyry and epithermal targets; intrusives are Eocene-aged (38.9-40.5 Ma), coeval with the world-class deposits to the east.
  • A 2024-2025 Phase 1 drill program completed 20 diamond holes (~8,200 m); an expanded EIA-Sd drill permit was approved Aug 10, 2026, clearing the way for a multi-target Phase 2 program.
  • No NI 43-101 mineral resource has been defined; mineralization to date is from historical drilling (Fierrazo) and surface channel sampling (Nioc).
Corporate & Strategy
  • Spun out of Auryn Resources (now Fury Gold Mines) to Auryn shareholders in Oct 2020; management has a track record of discovery and monetization (Cayden->Agnico Eagle C$205M; Keegan->Galiano).
  • Strategy: advance Sombrero via disciplined permitting/community work, then diversify jurisdiction (U.S. asset acquisition in progress via Coppernico USA Inc.).
  • Strategic shareholder Teck Resources holds 9.9% with two-year pro-rata participation and top-up rights.

2Core Assets
  • Sombrero is a single, large land package (~57,000 ha) with multiple target areas; the most advanced are Ccascabamba (incl. Fierrazo) and Nioc, complemented by a growing regional pipeline.
  • Mineralization style is Cu-Au skarn (exoskarn in Ferrobamba limestone) with porphyry and epithermal potential; historic drilling and channel sampling confirm broad, high-grade surface mineralization.
Sombrero — Ccascabamba / Fierrazo
Fierrazo is a brownfield magnetite-rich skarn, ~700 m long, with historical Aceros drilling (2013) returning up to 116 m @ 0.42% Cu + 0.24 g/t Au. First target to be drill-tested in Phase 2.
Sombrero — Nioc
Channel sampling defines a >1.5 km skarn-porphyry system; Zone 2 returned 70.1 m @ 0.92% Cu + 0.25 g/t Au + 2.45 g/t Ag, Zone 1 155 m @ 0.54% Cu. Coincident magnetic/gravity/chargeability anomalies, untested by drilling.
Sombrero — Tipicancha
Epithermal-porphyry system with >4 km alteration footprint, magnetic low and advanced-argillic core; vectoring toward a potential porphyry source at depth.
Sombrero — Antapampa & regional pipeline
Includes Good Lucky, Uchuy, Totora, Macha Machay, Milpoc, Ccello, Rumi and others; defined via geophysics, mapping and geochemistry across the 10 km intrusive cluster.
Sombrero — Good Lucky (Mollecruz option)
Mollecruz concessions (1,300 ha, host to Good Lucky) under option (100% + 0.5% NSR) but access blocked by force majeure since 2019; company continues community engagement.
U.S. asset acquisition
Coppernico USA Inc. incorporated Jun 2026; early-stage grassroots claims acquisition under negotiation for jurisdiction diversification (deferred acquisition costs C$29,891).
