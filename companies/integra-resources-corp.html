1Overview
  • Integra is a growing precious-metals producer in the Great Basin (USA) with one operating mine (Florida Canyon, NV) and two advanced heap-leach development projects: DeLamar (ID, FS 2025) and Nevada North (NV, PEA 2023).
  • Florida Canyon was acquired in 2024 for US$68M; the 2026 updated FS extends mine life to 2033, lifts reserves +74% to 1,191 koz, averages ~82 koz/yr, and delivers after-tax NPV5% of US$601M (base) / US$723M (spot).
  • DeLamar's 2025 Feasibility Study shows after-tax NPV5% of US$774M (base, US$3,000/oz Au) / US$1.9B (current, US$4,500/oz Au), 97% IRR, US$389M initial capex, a 10-year mine life and ~106 koz AuEq/yr.
  • The Company holds a peer-leading inventory of 7.7 Moz AuEq M&I and 4.3 Moz AuEq Inferred across the three assets.
  • Cross-check: the June 2026 Mineral Reserves & Resources statement and the July 2026 corporate presentation are consistent (Florida Canyon P&P 1,191 koz Au; DeLamar P&P 1,259 koz Au / 52.3 Moz Ag).
Investment thesis
  • Production cash flow from Florida Canyon self-funds the DeLamar / Nevada North growth pipeline
  • Scarcity of advanced, permitted US heap-leach assets in the current permitting environment
  • Clear path to a 250–300 koz AuEq multi-asset Great Basin platform
