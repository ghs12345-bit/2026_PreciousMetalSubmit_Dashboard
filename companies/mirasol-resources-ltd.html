1Overview
  • Mirasol Resources Ltd. (TSX-V: MRZ; OTC: MRZLF) is a project generator focused on copper, gold and silver discoveries in the mineral-rich regions of Chile and Argentina.
  • The flagship is the 100%-owned Sobek Cu-Au-Ag porphyry in Chile's Vicuña District, strategically located 7 km west of Filo del Sol (BHP/Lundin) and 3 km SW of NGEx's Lunahuasi.
  • Mirasol operates a project-generator model, retaining a large portfolio of grassroots-to-advanced projects and advancing select assets (e.g. Sobek) with self-funded exploration while seeking partners for the remainder.
Operating model
  • Self-funded Sobek exploration; broader portfolio (Virginia, Claudia, Gorbea Belt, Zeus, etc.) available for partnership/option.
  • 2026 field season at Sobek completed with target refinement and two 46 South drill holes.
2Core Assets
  • Sobek (Chile, Vicuña District): 100%-owned 9,640 ha Cu-Au-Ag porphyry (plus 4,600 ha 80% earn-in Rosita with SQM). Located 7 km west of Filo del Sol. 2026 drilling at 46 South confirmed a structurally controlled hydrothermal system; VN-Zone IP highlights a resistivity contrast increasing with depth; Green Wall shows high-grade copper surface samples (up to 14.7% Cu, 131 g/t Ag). No resource yet.
  • Virginia (Santa Cruz, Argentina): LSE silver project (74,429 ha) under a definitive option agreement with Silver Sands Resources Corp.
  • Broad portfolio across Chile & Argentina: Claudia, Sascha-Marcelina, La Curva, Homenaje, Libanesa (Argentina, Santa Cruz); Altazor, Coronación, Gorbea Belt, Rubi, Osiris, Zeus (Chile, Mio-Pliocene belt) — largely available for partnership.
