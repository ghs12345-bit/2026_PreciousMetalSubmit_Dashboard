  • Flagship AntaKori is a large, high-grade Cu-Au-Ag skarn/epithermal system in the world-class Miocene Au-Cu belt of northern Peru (Cajamarca), immediately adjacent to Coimolache's operating Tantahuatay mine.
  • Substantial 2019 resource: Indicated 250 Mt @ 0.74% CuEq (0.48% Cu, 0.29 g/t Au, 7.5 g/t Ag) and Inferred 267 Mt @ 0.66% CuEq — ~517 Mt total with strong Cu-Au-Ag credits and low strip.
  • Major de-risking milestone: an integrated Coimolache sulphide / AntaKori Mineral Resource Estimate was completed by SRK Peru on June 30, 2026 (first phase of the Coimolache collaboration), with an option to advance to a PEA.
  • Strong strategic backing: Osisko Gold Royalties (NSR + buy-back rights, US$5M in 2022), a Nuton (Rio Tinto) bio-leaching partnership showing attractive copper-extraction rates, and the Coimolache JV — all reduce dilution and technical risk.
  • Clean balance sheet for an explorer: ~C$8.3M cash and C$3.7M working capital as of Mar 31, 2026 funds ongoing programs.
Key risks
  • Pre-production explorer with no revenue; value depends on converting resources to a PEA/study, permitting and eventual project financing for a large capital project.
  • Jurisdiction / social-license risk in Peru: although adjacent to an operating mine, a new major development still requires community consent and permitting.
  • Arsenic content in the concentrate is elevated (avg ~520–860 ppm As) — metallurgical/marketing and smelter-penalty risk must be managed (Nuton bio-leaching is being tested to address this).
  • Resource is Indicated/Inferred (not reserves); the 2019 estimate is the filed basis, with the integrated MRE figures not yet publicly released in detail.
  • Dilution risk: ongoing financings and option grants (2.175M options at C$4.18 granted Jul 2026) increase the share count (~125.5M shares outstanding).

•Project Map
1Overview
  • Regulus is a Canadian copper-gold exploration and development company whose flagship is the AntaKori Cu-Au-Ag project in the Cajamarca region of northern Peru, ~50 km northwest of the city of Cajamarca.
  • AntaKori is a high-grade, low-strip skarn/epithermal system immediately adjacent to Coimolache's operating Tantahuatay oxide mine (Southern Copper / Buenaventura / ESPRO) — a proven mining district.
  • Shares trade on TSX-V (REG) and OTCQX (RGLSF); the Company pursues a consolidation-and-advance strategy via partnerships with Osisko, Nuton (Rio Tinto) and Coimolache.
Strategic rationale
  • Tier-1 Peruvian copper belt with existing infrastructure and an operating neighbouring mine — lowers development and permitting risk.
  • Large, high-grade resource with meaningful gold and silver by-product credits.
  • Partnerships (Osisko royalty, Nuton bio-leaching, Coimolache JV) bring technical expertise and non-dilutive funding.
2Core Assets
  • AntaKori (100% Regulus concessions; Cajamarca, Peru): NI 43-101 resource (Wood, effective Feb 22, 2019; report Apr 2019) at a 0.30% CuEq cut-off. Indicated 250 Mt @ 0.74% CuEq (0.48% Cu, 0.29 g/t Au, 7.5 g/t Ag) containing ~2.6 Blb Cu, 2.3 Moz Au, 61 Moz Ag; Inferred 267 Mt @ 0.66% CuEq (0.41% Cu, 0.26 g/t Au, 7.8 g/t Ag) containing ~2.4 Blb Cu, 2.2 Moz Au, 67 Moz Ag. Elevated arsenic (~520–860 ppm As) is a metallurgical consideration.
  • Integrated MRE: under a July 2024 collaboration agreement with Compañía Minera Coimolache S.A., an integrated Coimolache sulphide / AntaKori Mineral Resource Estimate was completed by SRK Peru on June 30, 2026; the parties may elect to advance to a PEA. Detailed figures are to be released on mutual agreement and regulatory approval.
  • Nuton (Rio Tinto) bio-leaching: Phase-Two metallurgical testwork (update May 2025) reported attractive copper-extraction rates, a potential processing route for the higher-arsenic material.
  • Other interests: Fireweed and Hualgayoc exploration prospects in Canada and Peru; a 2021 option to earn up to 60% of Gold Fields' claims adjacent to AntaKori to deepen/push back the resource pit.
