1Overview
  • ATEX Resources Inc. is a Vancouver-headquartered copper-gold exploration company whose sole material asset is the 100%-owned Valeriano Cu-Au porphyry project in Chile's Atacama region.
  • The company has no revenue and no production. It is a single-asset, pre-economic-study explorer funded entirely by equity, with a 31 October fiscal year-end.
  • In 2026 ATEX moved up two market tiers: TSX main board (effective 2026-04-27, ticker unchanged ATX) and OTCQX Best Market in the US (2026-05-12, ATXRF).
  • The September 2025 SRK maiden-update MRE established Valeriano as a district-scale system of roughly 1.99 Bt, of which 475 Mt sits in the Indicated category.
  • Strategy is straightforward: keep drilling to grow and upgrade the resource, submit environmental groundwork in parallel, and defer economic studies until an updated MRE lands in late 2027.
Positioning
  • Peer positioning is as a 'tonnage plus optionality' story rather than a near-term developer — there is no capex or NPV to anchor a valuation.
  • The high-grade porphyry core and the B2B domain are the company's answer to the classic criticism of large low-grade porphyries, offering a potential selective-mining entry point.
  • Presence of Agnico Eagle and Pierre Lassonde on the register is widely read as strategic validation of the asset.
Key caveat on district figures
  • Company presentations show a district panel combining Valeriano (~2.0 Bt) with 'El Encierro 0.5 Bt Inferred MRE — 2 Bt Global'.
  • The Q2 2026 MDA states plainly that El Encierro is held by Antofagasta / Barrick Gold and is NOT an ATEX asset.
  • Only Valeriano (and the unencumbered Nuevo Horizonte surface targets) should be attributed to ATEX.

  • Nuevo Horizonte — adjacent surface targets acquired by ATEX, carrying no NSR burden; no resource defined.
  • No mineral reserves exist, and no PEA / PFS / FS has been published, so mining method, throughput, recovery and capital cost remain undefined.

5Company Profile
  • Single-asset copper-gold explorer listed on the TSX (ATX) and OTCQX (ATXRF), with a 31 October fiscal year-end.
  • 2025 was the resource year: the SRK MRE with a 2025-09-23 effective date converted three years of drilling into 475 Mt of Indicated material.
  • 2026 has been a re-rating and governance year — TSX and OTCQX graduations, a first sustainability report, a royalty reduction, and two senior-officer changes.
  • The royalty transaction in June 2026 is unusually shareholder-friendly for a junior: ATEX sold a 10% interest in SCMV for roughly C$2.4M cash and simultaneously cut the Valeriano NSR from 2.5% to 2.3%, retaining a right of first refusal.
  • Environmental groundwork is underway ahead of studies, with a DIA filed 2026-04-23 declaring a US$140M investment across 2028–2033.
