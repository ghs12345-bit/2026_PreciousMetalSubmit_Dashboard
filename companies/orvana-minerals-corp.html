1Overview
  • Orvana is an Ontario-registered, multi-mine gold-copper-silver producer with three wholly-owned operations: Orovalle (El Valle) in northern Spain, Don Mario in Bolivia, and the Taguas exploration property in Argentina.
  • El Valle (Spain) is the established cash-generative engine; Don Mario is being restarted via an oxide-stockpile leach/SX-EW project; Taguas is a growth/exploration asset.
  • Shares trade on TSX (ORV) and OTCQX (ORVMF). As of Feb 2026 the Company holds 100% voting rights and board control of all three subsidiaries.
Operating model
  • Two operating jurisdictions (Spain producing, Bolivia restarting) plus one exploration frontier (Argentina).
  • By-product credited: silver treated as a by-product of gold; copper a co-product at El Valle and a separate cathode product at Don Mario.
