1Overview
  • Midnight Sun Mining Corp. (TSX-V: MMA; OTCQX: MDNGF) is a Zambia-focused copper exploration company whose 100%-owned Solwezi Project lies in the Domes Region of northwest Zambia.
  • The Solwezi Project comprises the Kazhiba (oxide + sulphide copper) and Dumbwa (near-surface sulphide copper) deposits, surrounded by major operating copper mines.
  • The Company is advancing a large-scale, district-scale copper exploration thesis anchored by a maiden resource at Kazhiba and a rapidly expanding Dumbwa system.
Location advantage
  • Solwezi is flanked to the north by First Quantum's Kansanshi mine and to the west by Barrick's Lumwana mine.
  • The Crunch Zone EM anomaly extends >5 km and occurs in the same stratigraphy as neighbouring Kansanshi.
2Core Assets
  • Kazhiba Main: maiden NI 43-101 Indicated oxide resource of 2.33 Mt @ 1.41% Cu (cut-off 0.10% Cu; effective Jan 20, 2026). Underlying basement domes analogous to Kansanshi/Sentinel/Lumwana host highly prospective sulphide copper targets (historic intercepts up to 11.3m @ 5.71% Cu).
  • Dumbwa: near-surface sulphide copper system confirmed over 6.7 km of strike (260+ holes, 56,855 m drilled as of Jun 2026); central higher-grade zone 200-600m wide. Phase One testing up to 11.5 km strike expected to conclude Q3 2026. Select intercepts: 1.03% Cu over 15m; 1.71% Cu over 8m; 0.46% Cu over 50m.
  • Crunch Zone: a >5 km continuous EM conductive trend in the same stratigraphy as Kansanshi, largely untested — a potential new discovery.
