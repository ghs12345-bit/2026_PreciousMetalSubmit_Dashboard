1Overview
  • Alpha Exploration Ltd. (TSX-V: ALEX) is a pure exploration company built around the Kerkasha licence in the Gash-Barka region of Eritrea, on the Arabian-Nubian Shield (ANS).
  • The investment case is a discovery case, not a production or development case: the company has no resource, no reserves, no revenue and no permitted mine.
  • Three deposit styles have been discovered by the in-house team since field work began in April 2018 - orogenic gold (Aburna), Cu-Au porphyry (Anagulu) and VMS (Tolegimja) - with the Anagulu discovery hole drilled in January 2020 and the TSX-V listing completed on 1 November 2021.
  • Corporate strategy is explicit and unusually honest: sell any discovery on completion of a bankable feasibility study rather than build a mine.
  • Note on scale: the deck and all 2026 news releases describe Kerkasha as 514 km², while the website Projects page states 771 km² - we have used 514 km² as the number Alpha itself repeats in regulated disclosure.
Why the Arabian-Nubian Shield
  • Over 1,000 recorded ancient copper and gold mines and 6,000+ prospects/occurrences, but only a handful in production or development.
  • Multiple proven deposit styles (porphyry, orogenic, VMS, epithermal) in the same belt, which is what allows one licence to host three different discovery types.
  • Alpha's thesis is that the ANS is under-explored relative to the Canadian and Australian shields and should therefore still contain undiscovered world-class camps.
Exploration approach
  • Machine learning and probabilistic modelling applied to inherited AngloGold-Ashanti/Thani airborne datasets plus Alpha's own soil and rock-chip geochemistry to generate targets remotely.
  • Field pXRF (Olympus Vanta M-series) in a company laboratory in Asmara allows real-time sampling and drill-programme adjustment, with QA/QC inserted every 25th sample.
  • Deliberate use of cheap reconnaissance methods first - termite-mound sampling, then shallow RAB (average 7.8m per hole) - before committing to RC and core metres.
2Core Assets
  • Kerkasha (Eritrea) is the only asset: 514 km², described as 100% Alpha-owned on the website, containing four areas of Colonial (Italian) workings and six artisanal gold prospects.
  • Aburna gold is the near-term value driver - a 7.2 x 2km gold-in-soil corridor with high-grade shallow intercepts including 18m @ 15.33 g/t Au (ABD012), 23m @ 6.74 g/t Au (ABD001), 49m @ 2.75 g/t Au (ABD013) and 16m @ 14.07 g/t Au (ABR037); approximately 90% of mineralised drilling sits above 120m and only ~20% of the corridor has been meaningfully drilled.
  • Anagulu is the scale option - a Cu-Au porphyry with AND001 returning 108m @ 1.24 g/t Au and 0.60% Cu (including 49m @ 2.42 g/t Au and 1.10% Cu) and AND005 109m @ 0.79 g/t Au and 0.35% Cu, inside an interpreted 4 x 2km footprint; H1-2026 work added Nightjar and expanded Camel, and ANRD016 delivered 67m @ 0.38% Cu and 0.10 g/t Au including 12m @ 1.20% Cu.
  • Tolegimja is the base-metal option - a >6km VMS trend with 26m @ 1.67% Zn, 0.38% Cu and 0.11 g/t Au (TLR009) and 18m @ 0.99% Zn, 0.34% Cu (TLR003) - currently the least-advanced of the three.
  • Cross-check caveat: because no NI 43-101 technical report is available for download, none of these numbers can be validated against a classified resource table; the deck's preliminary metallurgical claim of up to 91.9% gold recovery at Aburna is also pre-43-101 and should be treated as indicative only.
  • The chart below therefore does not show contained metal (none is defined). It shows grade-thickness of the best drilled intercept per project - gold in gram-metres, copper and zinc in percent-metres - as a like-for-like proxy for what has actually been intersected.

5Company Profile
  • Incorporated in the British Virgin Islands (registered office in Road Town, Tortola) with operations run from Asmara, Eritrea; established 2018 and listed on TSX-V on 1 November 2021.
  • Mission is narrow by design: discover world-class gold and base-metal deposits in the Arabian-Nubian Shield, then monetise at bankable feasibility rather than build.
  • Phase 1 (complete) acquired Kerkasha in January 2018 and used inherited AngloGold-Ashanti/Thani airborne data plus new geochemistry to generate the Aburna, Anagulu and Tolegimja discoveries.
  • Phase 2 (current) is expansion drilling on all three discoveries with the first maiden resource targeted at Aburna.
  • Phase 3 (stated intent) is a second Eritrean licence and/or expansion into the wider ANS - the deck specifically names Saudi Arabia, and the dashboard records Alpha's primary focus as Eritrea plus Saudi Arabia.
