Investment highlights
  • Haib is one of Africa's largest porphyry copper systems: a March 2026 MRE of 1,323 Mt at ~0.26% Cu containing ~3.48 Mt (7.66 Blb) copper, plus molybdenum and gold credits.
  • Simple, straightforward metallurgy (chalcopyrite-dominated) and close to existing Namibian infrastructure — supports a low-cost, long-life open-pit development.
  • Strong financial backing: ~C$70m cash (Jan 2026) and institutional/strategic support, including Teck as a ~5% strategic shareholder (ex-Rio Tinto/Teck pedigree).
  • Active PFS due end-2026 with a 55,000m infill/expansion drill programme; recent drilling returned some of the highest grades to date (8.01% Cu, 60.5 g/t Ag from surface).
  • Growth optionality via two Zambian Copperbelt licences (745 km2, 51% held with option to 80%) adjacent to major deposits such as Nchanga.
Key risks
  • Low grade and early stage: Haib is PEA-stage; economics and conversion to Reserves are unproven until the PFS/FS.
  • Large capital requirement for a bulk-tonnage porphyry; financing the build will require material new capital.
  • Single flagship asset concentration — Koryx value is overwhelmingly Haib-dependent.
  • Jurisdictional and permitting risk in Namibia (though mining-friendly) and early-stage exposure in Zambia.
  • Copper-price and FX sensitivity; pre-revenue, pre-cash-flow explorer.
Near-term catalysts
  • PFS publication targeted for end of 2026.
  • Ongoing 55,000m drill programme (infill + expansion) with results through 2026.
  • Potential upgrade of the March 2026 MRE as drilling converts Inferred to Indicated.
  • Zambia exploration field season and geochemistry results (2026).
  • Possible strategic financing / offtake ahead of project definition.

1Overview
  • Koryx Copper Inc (TSX-V: KRY; NSX: KYX; OTCQX: KRYXF) is a Canadian copper developer advancing its 100%-owned Haib copper-molybdenum-gold porphyry project in southern Namibia, alongside an exploration portfolio in Zambia's Copperbelt.
  • Haib is one of Africa's largest undeveloped porphyry copper systems, with >120,000 m of historical drilling by Falconbridge, Rio Tinto and Teck (a continuing strategic shareholder), and a March 2026 Mineral Resource Estimate of 1,323 Mt at ~0.26% Cu.
  • With copper demand driven by electrification, Koryx is de-risking Haib toward a Pre-Feasibility Study targeted for end-2026 and a potential low-cost, long-life open-pit operation.
Strategic positioning
  • Clean, simple share structure with institutional, strategic and HNW backing (≈26% institutions/strategics/HNW; Teck ≈5%; management ≈15%; other ≈54%).
  • Zambia licences (51%, option to 80%) provide district-scale exploration upside near Nchanga (>700 Mt @ ~2.5% Cu).
