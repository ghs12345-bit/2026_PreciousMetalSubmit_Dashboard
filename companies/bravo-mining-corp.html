Investment highlights
  • Large, high-grade PGM+Au+Ni resource: 10.4 Moz PdEq M&I (158 Mt @ 2.04 g/t) + 5.0 Moz PdEq Inferred (78 Mt @ 2.01 g/t) at 100%-owned Luanga, Carajas, Brazil — one of few large-scale PGM sources outside geopolitically challenged regions.
  • Robust PEA (Aug-2025): after-tax NPV(8%) US$1.25B (base, concentrate sales) / US$1.86B (alternate, vertical integration); IRR ~49%; payback 2.4 yrs; 17-year mine life; initial capex US$495.8M.
  • Exceptional infrastructure: low-cost hydro power (US$0.10/kWh), paved highways, rail, port, skilled workforce; anchor project in the Barcarena Export Processing Zone (ZPE).
  • Strong treasury: US$94.1M cash (Jun-2026) after H1-2026 raises (~C$115M gross); near-zero debt; well-funded through PFS/FS and initial works.
  • High insider alignment (Board/Management 42.7%) and backing by 39 institutional investors; experienced Brazil PGM/nickel team (ex-Avanco / OZ Minerals, Anglo Platinum, Vale).
Key risks
  • Pre-production developer; PEA is preliminary (no reserves) — economics depend on PGM price decks and the unproven vertical-integration case.
  • Single-asset concentration (Luanga) in Brazil; permitting/social licensing and the Installation Licence (LI) application pending completion of the PFS.
  • PFS/FS execution risk and potential capex escalation on a ~US$593M base-case project advancing from PEA.
  • PGM price volatility (Pd, Pt, Rh) and BRL/USD FX exposure; nickel/copper by-product credits uncertain.
  • Related-party concentration: CEO/Chairman Luis Azevedo and affiliated entities bill the company; key-person and governance risk.

1Overview
  • Bravo Mining is a Canadian- (BC) and Brazil-based mineral exploration & development company advancing its 100%-owned Luanga PGM + Au + Ni Project in the Carajas Mineral Province, Para, Brazil.
  • Luanga is a multi-million-ounce, Tier-1 PGM + Au + Ni deposit — one of the few large-scale sources of these critical metals outside geopolitically challenged regions, with outstanding infrastructure.
  • Following the 2025 MRE and PEA, Bravo is advancing toward a Pre-Feasibility Study (PFS) targeted for Q3 2026, with a DFS targeted Q3 2027; it is also opening a copper-gold (IOCG) exploration frontier in Carajas.
Asset & strategy
  • Luanga (100%): PGM+Au+Ni open-pit; Vale retained a 1% NSR; BNDES holds a 2% royalty on platinum-concentrate net revenue.
  • Copper-Gold (IOCG) division led by ex-Vale exploration director; early-stage Carajas copper-gold exploration with no resource yet.
